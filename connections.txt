LARKEN CIRCUIT CONNECTIONS
=========================

CONTROLLER
----------
ESP32-C3 SuperMini

DISPLAY MODULE
--------------
2.4-inch 240x320 SPI TFT combo board, currently ILI9341. The photographed
module's J2 header, viewed from the rear with J2 at the bottom and labels
readable, is left-to-right:

T_IRQ, T_DO, T_DIN, T_CS, T_CLK, SDO(MISO), LED, SCK, SDI(MOSI), DC,
RESET, CS, GND, VCC

Connect the LCD signals and power as follows:

Display J2 SCK       -> ESP32-C3 GPIO 4
Display J2 SDI/MOSI  -> ESP32-C3 GPIO 6
Display J2 CS       -> ESP32-C3 GPIO 7
Display J2 DC       -> ESP32-C3 GPIO 3
Display J2 RESET    -> ESP32-C3 GPIO 10
Display J2 SDO/MISO -> Not connected
Display J2 VCC      -> 5 V supply (user-reported module operation)
Display J2 LED      -> 5 V supply (user-reported module operation)
Display J2 GND      -> Common GND

The display's touch-controller pins (T_IRQ, T_DO, T_DIN, T_CS, T_CLK) and
microSD socket are not connected to this firmware.

TOUCH SENSORS
-------------
TTP223 T1 VCC -> ESP32-C3 3V3
TTP223 T1 GND -> Common GND
TTP223 T1 OUT -> ESP32-C3 GPIO 0

TTP223 T2 VCC -> ESP32-C3 3V3
TTP223 T2 GND -> Common GND
TTP223 T2 OUT -> ESP32-C3 GPIO 1

POWER BUTTON
------------
Normally-open momentary pushbutton between ESP32-C3 GPIO 5 and GND.
Optional external pull-up: 10 kOhm from GPIO 5 to 3V3.

POWER AND DECOUPLING
--------------------
ESP32-C3 GND, TFT GND, both TTP223 GND pins, and supply negative -> Common GND
10 uF capacitor positive lead -> regulated supply rail
10 uF capacitor negative lead -> Common GND

Use the polarity and voltage ratings printed on the capacitor. ESP32-C3 GPIO
signals are 3.3 V only. Do not connect 5 V to any ESP32-C3 GPIO or 3V3 rail.
